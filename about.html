About

Private aviation, a little more within reach.

JetDrop helps travellers discover discounted private jet empty-leg flights across Europe — and get told when one matches their airports and budget.

What we do

Empty-leg opportunities are scattered across operators and marketplaces. JetDrop brings them into one simple place and lets you set alerts, so you don’t have to check dozens of sites.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t operate aircraft.
  • We aren’t a charter broker and don’t issue flight tickets.
  • We don’t take payment for flights — you book with the provider.

How we’ll earn trust

Every listing will show its original source, a last-checked time and an honest availability status. We won’t invent urgency, reviews, discounts or operator confirmations.

Where we are today

This website is an early build. Listings are clearly labelled samples. Real flight data, alerts and memberships will be switched on only once the underlying services are connected and tested.
