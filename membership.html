What you should know before subscribing

  • Monthly recurring billing. JetDrop Plus would renew every month at €9.99 until you cancel.
  • Cancellation. You will be able to cancel at any time from your account; access continues to the end of the paid period. Full terms are in the Subscription & Cancellation Policy.

  • Flights are not included. Every flight is booked and paid for separately with the aircraft operator or authorized booking provider.
